Product Architecture

Designed for accurate billing and clean data boundaries

Validated Invoice Lifecycle & Filtering

Create and edit invoices across DRAFT, SENT, PAID, OVERDUE, and CANCELLED statuses with server-enforced invoice number uniqueness, date-range validation, positive decimal precision, and paginated search.

True Financial Metrics

Separates settled paid revenue from total invoiced, outstanding, overdue, draft, and cancelled amounts—never mislabeling drafts or cancelled records as revenue.

Referential Client Integrity

Protects historical invoice records by blocking client deletion when invoices exist, while syncing portal account emails cleanly on updates.

Print-Ready PDF Exports (Full-Stack & Browser Demo)

Generates structured single-invoice PDFs and multi-invoice client statements via iText 7 on the Spring Boot backend, and mirrors the layout in browser-local demo mode via jsPDF with zero external API calls.

Role Separation

Two purpose-built workspaces with strict boundaries

Business Workspace

ROLE_USER
  • • Dashboard KPIs, monthly trends, and status distribution charts
  • • Full invoice CRUD, search, status filtering, sorting, and PDF export
  • • Client directory, invoice history, and deletion protection
  • • Invitation generation, regeneration, email delivery, and portal block/unblock

Client Self-Service Portal

CLIENT Token
  • • Activation via 8-character invitation code and client email
  • • Read-only visibility strictly scoped to the client's own invoices
  • • Individual invoice and full statement PDF downloads
  • • Safe contact profile updates (email and portal status remain locked)
